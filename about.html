About Pinnacle Shield Insurance

Founded in 1985, Pinnacle Shield Insurance has been a trusted name in personal and family insurance for over four decades. What started as a small office in Hartford, Connecticut has grown into a nationwide provider serving customers in all 50 states. Our mission has always been simple: to make dependable insurance coverage accessible, affordable, and easy to understand.

At Pinnacle Shield, we believe that insurance should be transparent and customer-focused. That’s why we offer flexible coverage options for auto, home, and life insurance backed by responsive support and straightforward pricing. Our team of licensed professionals works hard to help individuals and families find protection that fits their needs without unnecessary complexity.

Over the years, we’ve helped protect millions of policyholders and built a reputation for reliability, integrity, and service. Headquartered in Hartford, CT, Pinnacle Shield continues to combine decades of experience with modern technology to deliver fast quotes, efficient claims support, and peace of mind for customers across the country.
